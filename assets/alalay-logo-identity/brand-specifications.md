# Alalay Logo Identity

Selected direction: **Concept 1 — Guided Wallet**.

The rounded outer form reads as a stable financial container. The cut geometry creates an abstract capital A, while the interior route rises gently to communicate guided, controlled progress. It remains friendly and useful rather than institutional or investment-focused.

## Colors

- Deep emerald: `#0F6F57`
- Primary green: `#0F8A6B`
- Supporting teal: `#159A8A`
- Soft mint: `#D8EFE2`
- Warm off-white: `#F8F7F2`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

## Safe area

- Master symbol canvas: 512 × 512 units.
- Visible geometry: x=112–400, y=80–432.
- Minimum clear space: 64 units (12.5% of canvas) around the optical silhouette.
- Android adaptive foreground: symbol occupies 56.25% of the 1024px canvas width and 68.75% of its height, centered.
- Recommended minimum digital size: 24px; favicon supplied at 32px.
- Monochrome notification artwork uses a single solid white mark on transparency.

## Wordmark

Recommended editable face: Nunito Sans Bold, with Arial as fallback. `logo-horizontal-editable.svg` preserves live text. Distribution lockups retain the same editable SVG structure in this draft; convert text to outlines in the production design tool after the wordmark typeface is confirmed/licensed.
